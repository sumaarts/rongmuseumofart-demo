Careers

The Róng Museum of Art is committed to building a learning-oriented, collaborative, diverse, and inclusive team. We value different backgrounds, perspectives, experiences, and voices. We embrace curiosity, encourage participation, and prioritize expression. We hope all team members can learn from one another, grow together, and realize their potential in an open and supportive work environment.

If you share our passion for contemporary art and cultural communication and believe that art has the power to connect and inspire people, we welcome you to apply to join the Róng Museum of Art. By becoming an integral part of our endeavor, you can help drive forward diverse dialogues and bring art into more people’s lives.

To apply: Please send your résumé to hr@rongmuseumofart.org. For any questions regarding the positions or the application process, the recruitment team can also be reached via the same email address.

Responsibilities:

1. Operations System Construction: Establish and optimize the museum’s operations systems and processes, regularly evaluate and optimize operations systems, ensure operational compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

2. Interdepartmental Coordination and Integration: Coordinate with departments such as legal and finance to optimize resource allocation, work closely with the marketing team in integrating external resources and expanding audience outreach as well as partnerships.

3. Budget and Procurement Management: Prepare and monitor operating budgets, formulate procurement strategies and plans, ensure the timely arrival of operational materials.

4. Data and Metrics Management: Integrate and analyze operating data and metrics to support decision-making processes, manage the Museum’s archives and project schedules.

5. Operations Support: Manage and develop the operations team, organize training, supervise the execution of management policies, coordinate daily operations, respond to emergencies as they arise, improve audience experience.

6. Fundraising and Development: Cultivate relationships with corporate and individual donors, as well as other prospective partners, develop sponsorship proposals aligned with national cultural and tourism policies, secure philanthropic donations and corporate sponsorships.

7. Retail Operations Management: Design and implement admissions and membership growth strategies, oversee the operations of the Museum shop, cafe, and other commercial facilities, ensure that all services align with the Museum’s mission and brand identity.

Requirements:

1. Educational Background: Bachelor’s degree or above. Majors in Arts Management, Cultural Industry Management, Marketing, and other relevant fields are preferred, as well as familiarity with Chinese policies relating to the fields of art, culture, and tourism.

2. Previous Experience: At least 7 years of experience in development, fundraising, or cultural project management. Skills in building and maintaining key stakeholder relationships and corporate relationships. Work experience in art or cultural institutions is preferred.

3. Core Competencies: Strong commercial acumen and the ability to independently develop partnership proposals and lead negotiations. Outstanding project management and multitasking skills with an attention to detail. Fluency in written and spoken Chinese and English, with excellent report-writing skills.

4. Professional Qualities: A strong commitment to the public value of arts and culture, with a high standard of professional integrity and sense of discretion. Ability to thrive in a fast-paced work environment, work effectively under pressure, and foster efficient interdepartmental collaboration.

About us

The Róng Museum of Art presents contemporary art and intermedia practices through a global lens.

Located in Shenzhen, China, the Róng Museum of Art is dedicated to showcasing and collecting a wide range of contemporary creative practices, including visual art, moving image, performance, and digital media. By focusing on the arts of our time, the Museum is committed to critically examining its role as a cultural institution, positioning itself at the intersection of history and the future whilefostering global dialogue and cross-cultural exchange.

At the heart of the Museum lies a core value: “interlinkedness” (gong rong). Rong is a Chinese character with ancient and layered connotations, embracing meanings such as melding (rong hua), integration (rong he), fusion (rong hui), and exchange (rong tong). This single character encapsulates our new vision of a cultural institution for the twenty-first century. In an era of globalization, interlinkedness, gong rong, signifies not only mutual respect, but also an active understanding of one another, mutual learning, and collective growth.

The Róng Museum of Art aspires not only to offer inspiring cultural experiences, but also to serve as an open, diverse, and trusted learning organization and knowledge-sharing platform, fostering the sustainable development of culture.

Occupying a space of over 4,000 square meters, the Róng Museum of Art is expected to open to the public in the second half of 2027.

“Through its exhibitions and programs, the Róng Museum of Art is committed to activating a new perspective for its audiences: to view ever-changing life as an ongoing act of artmaking. Each person’s life carries the potential to become a unique work of art, one that resonates with and inspires others, so that together we may create a world of possibilities. ” —— Pi Li

Architecture

This is a building that aims to spark curiosity, draw people in, and inspire a sense of wonder.

The Róng Museum of Art is an architectural extension of the idea of sustainability and landscape, while also being a building dedicated to culture, experimentation, and exploration.

The Museum space is elevated to form a series of cone-shaped pavilions with a very small footprint, freeing up the ground plane as a public space and entrance to the building.

Spatial Design

The Róng Museum of Art is an iconic building within the M80 urban complex where art, architecture, and urban life converge.

Guided by the Museum’s vision of “interconnectedness” ( gongrong ) and responding to Shenzhen’s dense yet porous urban fabric, the design provides a framework for exhibition, learning, and public engagement. Its galleries are arranged within a dynamic, daylit perimeter that encourages circulation and contemplation and echoes the layered network of streets, canals, parks, and elevated walkways of the surrounding Houhai neighborhood.

About M80

Located in Shenzhen’s Houhai neighborhood, M80 is a mixed-use urban complex that brings together work, life, culture, commerce, leisure, and nature. Envisioned as “an ideal place for countless conversations,” and shaped by architect Ole Scheeren’s fluid architectural language, it fosters interpersonal encounters and forges a connection to both the surrounding community and the city at large. The Róng Museum of Art sits in the heart of M80 and forms an integral part of its cultural ecosystem.
